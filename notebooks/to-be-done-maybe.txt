I would like to continue working on my rational hypercomplex class, Hy, in my public repo: https://github.com/alreich/hyper_rationals. Currently, the Hy class uses the Cayley-Dickson construction to recursively represent rational complex numbers, rational quaternions, rational octonions, and so on. Without making any edits to the existing code at this time, I would like to discuss the possibility of also representing the following types of mult-dimensional rational numbers:

* split-complex numbers
* split-quaternions
* split-biquaternions
* Hurwitz quaternions
* split-octonions
